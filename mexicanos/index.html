How to play

  1. 90s debate — read the question, all teams talk at once.
  2. Write top 3 secretly on your sheet (1 = best guess).
  3. ¡Tiempo! — everyone shows their sheet, then reveal answers on TV lowest → highest.
  4. Tap a team's icon on the revealed answer to award its points (tap again to undo). #1 = 50 · #2 = 30 · #3 = 20 · #4 = 10 · #5 = 5
